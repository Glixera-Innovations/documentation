## Boost Perks
-> Special access to exclusive channels<br />
-> Exclusive peeks sent only to boosters<br />
-> Access to send any link anywhere (cannot be malicious link)<br />
-> Access to have one custom role that shows above your role
## Booster Terms of Service (BTOS)
### Booster Peeks
You are explicitly not allowed to leak any booster peeks to any other non boosters in the community. Doing so will result in your access from Glixera Innovations being revoked.

### Custom Roles
Your own custom role must comply to the general rules, including impersonation and many more.

### Rules Exemption
Boosting our server does not mean that you will come without any punishments, you get same moderation action as regular members.
